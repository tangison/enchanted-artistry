# Product Definition

## Project

**Enchanted Artistry landing page**

Legal entity: **ENCHANTED ARTISTRY CC**

Mode: public website refinement, GitHub-to-Vercel deployment connected

## Product statement

Enchanted Artistry is a purpose-driven umbrella brand connecting beauty, creativity, confidence, mentorship and future community impact. The landing page introduces that wider vision while giving current service categories and the founders a clear, trusted presence.

## Audience

- Women interested in beauty, confidence and self-expression.
- Parents, young people and community partners interested in future creative mentorship.
- Creative collaborators and prospective clients.

## Objective and conversion

Help a first-time visitor understand the brand in under 30 seconds and invite a direct email or WhatsApp enquiry.

## Confirmed facts

- Enchanted Artistry is the umbrella brand.
- Current public offer combines cosmetology, creativity and caring guidance.
- Purpose: celebrate beauty, inspire creativity, build confidence and encourage self-expression.
- Legal name: ENCHANTED ARTISTRY CC.
- Founders: Anthea Jade Feris and Chané Yvette Philander.
- Service directions: makeup, hair, drama coaching, guidance and content creation.
- Guidance is focused on mothers through pregnancy, post-partum and breastfeeding.
- Content creation covers creative content, design and meaningful storytelling, not photography.
- Future direction includes youth mentorship, creative arts, content creation and media experience.

## Boundaries

- Enchanted Hands is excluded from this page at the user's direction.
- Future initiatives are not described as currently enrolling or operating.
- No fake booking, shop, journal, portfolio, social account or newsletter workflow.
- No founder personal mobile numbers are published.

## Success criteria

- Visually recognisable against the approved reference without copying unusable screenshot pixels into production.
- Authentic founder portraits remain intact. The original logo remains archived and the website uses the client-authorised descriptor correction.
- Clear service and umbrella-brand story on desktop and mobile.
- Responsive, keyboard accessible, indexable and production-build clean.
