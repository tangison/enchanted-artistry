# Enchanted Artistry Brand Direction

## Approved visual story

The client-approved screenshot combines a pale blush field, soft floral corners, rose calls to action, lavender depth, fine gold accents, refined serif typography and expressive script. The local build translates that direction into a responsive, accessible system while keeping the official logo untouched.

## Personality

- Feminine, assured and warm.
- Faith-rooted and purpose-driven.
- Creative and detailed without becoming cluttered.
- Premium but approachable.

## Logo preservation

| Asset | Use |
| --- | --- |
| `assets/logos/enchanted-artistry-original.jpg` | Untouched client-supplied master |
| `assets/logos/enchanted-artistry-transparent.png` | Default website logo |
| `assets/logos/enchanted-artistry-white.png` | Exact artwork on white |
| `assets/logos/enchanted-artistry-black.png` | Exact artwork on near-black |

Never stretch, crop, recolour, retype, redraw, trace, bevel or convert the logo into an SVG. Preserve `CC`, the descriptor line, open rings, lettering and original proportions.

## Typography

- **Bodoni Moda**: editorial headings and display type; echoes the high-contrast wordmark.
- **Manrope**: navigation, body, labels and interface; keeps detailed layouts readable.
- **Allura**: approved expressive script moments only; used for calling, founder accents and the closing verse.

## Composition

- Floral hero with deliberate open space for brand and message.
- Fine uppercase labels and high-contrast serif section titles.
- A four-item values strip, five rounded service cards and two substantial founder cards.
- Controlled sparkles, heart rules and botanical detail consistent with the reference.
- Alternating ivory, blush and lavender-to-rose sections.

## Image direction

- Founder portraits are real supplied images and take priority.
- Service images are generated still-life or anonymous illustrative category images.
- No generated person is presented as staff, founder, client or participant.
- Avoid invented signage, text, credentials and programme evidence.

## Accessibility

- Long-form copy uses dark ink on ivory or white.
- Decorative rose is not used for long body text.
- Interactive states remain visible and keyboard operable.
- Reduced-motion mode removes nonessential transitions.
